# Results

Small computed results committed with their provenance (spec §12). Every file here is
regenerated by a command named in its `provenance.generatedBy`; none is hand-edited.

| Path | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| `forces/default.json` | Muscle insertion forces of the right eye on a 5 × 5 gaze lattice over ±30° (Fick x temporal, y up), from the TypeScript eye model: insertion point, line of action at the insertion, net pull direction, tangent point, arc of contact, force (N), insertion width, tendon elongation. Schema `eye-forces/1`, EOM frame. | `cd web && pnpm --filter @eye/eye-model export-forces --grid 5 --range 30 --out ../data/results/forces/default.json` |

Forces are `activation × maxForceN` with the length-proxy activation (an approximation, spec
§4.4 v1) and the force capacities of `data/eom/forces.json` (partly assumed). At the lattice
corners the antagonists relax to zero force.
